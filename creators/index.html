How commission works

A referral signup creates attribution only and earns no commission by itself. Commission becomes payable only after the referred customer purchases Unhinged Nav, the applicable Apple/Google payment period has passed, and Unhinged Nav has actually received and cleared the revenue.

Creators follow the same platform payment delay that Unhinged Nav experiences. Refunds, reversals, chargebacks, taxes, and platform fees are excluded from commissionable net revenue. We do not promise a fixed 30-day payout when the applicable platform takes longer.

Commercial details will be set out in your agreement before enrollment. Demonstrate the app safely and describe your actual experience honestly.
