Unhinged Nav for iPhone is coming.

The iPhone version is planned after the Android beta reaches the right level of stability. The goal is the same Unhinged Nav experience across both platforms rather than a stripped-down iOS edition.

Planned iPhone experience

The iPhone release is intended to include voice-first navigation controls, route preview, live GPS guidance, destination search, Drive/Walk/Bike modes, and the Codependent, Sarcastic, and Adult personality choices.

Why Android is first

Testing one mobile platform first allows navigation, search, guidance timing, and voice-command behavior to be proven on real roads before expanding the beta. That reduces the chance of carrying unresolved navigation problems into a second platform.

CarPlay

Apple CarPlay integration is part of the longer-term cross-platform roadmap. It is not represented here as a currently released feature.
