Privacy

Privacy without the corporate fog.

Last updated: September 20, 2026. This page summarizes the current public website and navigation-app privacy approach while the product is in beta development.

Beta waitlist information

The public website collects the information you submit to the beta waitlist, including your name, email address, phone platform, country, and email consent. Campaign and referral information may also be recorded when it is present in the signup URL so we can understand which outreach brings testers to the site.

How waitlist information is used

Waitlist information is used to manage beta participation, send testing invitations and related product updates, distinguish Android and iPhone testers, prevent duplicate registrations, and understand signup sources. Unhinged Nav does not sell beta signup information.

Creator applications and referrals

Creator applications collect your name, business email, country, phone type, channel link, audience description, and contact consent so we can review participation and contact you. Referral codes supplied in links may be carried between pages during your visit and saved with a beta signup. We use them to connect qualifying purchases to creators and administer commissions. Creators are not given your signup email or private customer information. This referral feature uses no cookies or persistent browser storage. Contact hello@unhingednav.com for access, correction or deletion requests.

Location information in the navigation app

Navigation functions require location information for search, routing, route progress, and turn-by-turn guidance. Mapping and routing providers may process location information as needed to provide those functions.

Advertising

Unhinged Nav does not sell search placement and does not plan to interrupt active navigation with advertising. The public website does not use signup information to sell targeted advertising.

Security and abuse prevention

The signup service uses validation, duplicate-email controls, bot-trap fields, request-size limits, and rate limiting. Rate limiting uses a one-way representation of network information rather than storing a raw address in the signup table.

Questions or opt-out requests

Contact hello@unhingednav.com for privacy questions or to request that beta-update emails stop.
