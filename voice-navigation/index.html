Voice navigation

Use your voice. Touch the phone less.

Unhinged Nav is being built around the idea that common navigation controls should be available by voice, especially once a trip is underway.

Supported and tested voice actions

Repeat guidance

Ask the navigator to repeat the current instruction instead of searching the screen for the last turn.

Mute and unmute

Control spoken guidance without digging through menus.

Re-center the map

Return the map to the active route view after manual map movement.

Switch personality modes

Move between Codependent, Sarcastic, and Adult guidance during a trip.

Find stops along the route

Search for useful destinations such as coffee or gas without abandoning the active route.

Call a contact

Voice calling can prepare the contact and hand off to the phone dialer; the operating system may still require the final call action.

Why voice-first matters

Navigation is most useful when it communicates clearly without demanding constant visual interaction. Unhinged Nav prioritizes spoken guidance and voice-accessible controls while preserving a visual map for context.

Important: Voice control does not make distracted driving safe. Drivers are responsible for following local laws and operating the vehicle safely.
