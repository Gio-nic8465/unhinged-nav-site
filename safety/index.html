Driving safety

The personality is unhinged. The safety priorities are not.

Unhinged Nav is designed to make common navigation actions available with less phone interaction. Entertainment never outranks clear directions or safe driving behavior.

Safety design priorities

Preview before moving

Route preview lets users review the trip and upcoming steps before starting navigation.

Clear spoken directions

Personality can change the delivery, but critical navigation information still needs to remain understandable and timely.

Voice-accessible controls

Repeat, mute, unmute, re-center, mode switching, and supported searches are designed to reduce menu hunting during navigation.

Real-road testing

Navigation behavior is tested on actual routes, including cellular-only conditions, rather than relying only on stationary simulation.

Driver responsibility

No navigation app eliminates distraction or replaces safe judgment. Drivers should obey traffic laws, road signs, local restrictions, and instructions from public authorities. Do not interact with a phone in a way that is unsafe or illegal while driving.

Adult Mode is optional and intended for users 18 and older. Personality content should never obscure a safety-critical instruction.
