Android beta

Android beta: join the First Wave.

Android is the first public testing platform for Unhinged Nav. The beta is focused on real-road navigation quality, voice controls, search, route preview, and personality-mode behavior.

What beta testers help evaluate

  • Destination search and route selection
  • Route preview and step-by-step instructions
  • Live GPS guidance and arrival detection
  • Repeat, mute, unmute, and re-center voice controls
  • Codependent, Sarcastic, and Adult personality modes
  • Real-world behavior on cellular connections and during normal driving conditions

Your two-week testing commitment

We are recruiting U.S. Android testers aged 18 and older. Use your Google Play account email to sign up.

Remain opted in to the Google Play closed test for at least 14 consecutive days and keep the beta installed. Try the app regularly during those two weeks and send honest search, routing, navigation and voice feedback to hello@unhingednav.com.

Google Play closed testing is active. Eligible U.S. Android signups receive group and Google Play opt-in steps immediately on the confirmation screen. Signing up does not start the 14-day period. That period begins when you opt in through the Google Play testing link. Character voices require app-account activation after installation.

Set up routes while parked or as a passenger, and follow road signs and local laws.

What the waitlist means

After signup, eligible U.S. Android users can join the beta access group, opt in on Google Play, and follow the installation link. Google checks account and device eligibility. Use the Google account email you expect to use for Google Play testing.

First Wave benefit

First Wave beta testers are planned to receive three months free when the commercial service launches, subject to the final beta offer terms communicated to testers.
