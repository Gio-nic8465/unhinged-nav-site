Android beta

Android beta: join the First Wave.

Android is the first public testing platform for Unhinged Nav. The beta is focused on real-road navigation quality, voice controls, search, route preview, and personality-mode behavior.

What beta testers help evaluate

  • Destination search and route selection
  • Route preview and step-by-step instructions
  • Live GPS guidance and arrival detection
  • Repeat, mute, unmute, and re-center voice controls
  • Codependent, Sarcastic, and Adult personality modes
  • Real-world behavior on cellular connections and during normal driving conditions

What the waitlist means

Joining the waitlist is not an immediate app download. When a testing slot is available, Android testers receive the testing link and instructions by email. Use the Google account email you expect to use for Google Play testing.

First Wave benefit

First Wave beta testers are planned to receive three months free when the commercial service launches, subject to the final beta offer terms communicated to testers.
